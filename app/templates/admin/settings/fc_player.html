Remote-trigger a Fire TV / Android TV device to play DRM channels, captured back in by an HDMI encoder
Requires the FastChannels Player app installed on the device (ADB debugging must be turned on first). An HDMI encoder captures the device's decoded output for re-ingestion elsewhere (e.g. Channels DVR).
