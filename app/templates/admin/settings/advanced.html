Experimental output and troubleshooting
Rewrite stream timestamps in M3U Experimental
Adds tvc-stream-timestamps="rewrite" to generated M3U entries for Channels DVR. This may help recordings with bad durations or timestamp corruption, but can affect in-progress playback on some streams.
Debug logging
Adds extra diagnostic detail to the logs for modules that support it (currently: Spectrum sign-in). Off by default — turn this on only while reproducing a specific issue, since it's more verbose than normal; equivalent to setting the FC_DEBUG environment variable.
