Built-in capture Beta add-on
Plays DRM channels in Chrome inside FastChannels and captures them. No extra device, container or encoder.
Optional download (about 450 MB on your data volume): Google Chrome straight from Google, plus PulseAudio and FFmpeg. Each tuner uses about 2 CPU cores and 1 GB of memory at 720p, and capture runs at lower priority than the rest of FastChannels. Linux x86_64 only. Picture is software Widevine re-encoded, so for the best quality use FastChannels Player on an Android device.

Cuts capture CPU use several times over on Intel (Quick Sync) and AMD. Add devices: ["/dev/dri:/dev/dri"] to the FastChannels service in your compose file and recreate the container; the add-on installs the drivers itself and falls back to CPU if the GPU test fails. NVIDIA isn't supported.
Add it as an HLS custom channels source next to your regular playlist; it carries only the DRM channels. Set the source's stream limit to your tuner count.
