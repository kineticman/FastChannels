Single stream — one FastChannels Player device captured through one fixed HDMI encoder
Best for one concurrent stream. The FastChannels Player companion app runs on a Fire TV or Android TV device; its HDMI output is captured back into your DVR through a fixed encoder URL.
Enable hardware capture
Enables the FastChannels Player companion app used by HDMI Capture and ah4c Capture. It only applies to verified DRM sources: Sling, NBCUniversal TVE, PBS, Amazon Prime (free), DirecTV Stream, Vidaa, Philo, Roku, and Fubo.
adb port (:5555) is derived automatically. Required for HDMI Capture; also retained as a compatibility fallback when ah4c does not name a tuner device.
Install pushes the app over ADB; it requires the device IP to be saved and ADB debugging enabled on the device.
Stop playback when nobody's watching
For HDMI Capture, stop playback after about five minutes without Channels DVR activity or a FastChannels /watch heartbeat. Leave this off for third-party clients pointed straight at an M3U. ah4c stops its assigned device through its own disconnect hook.
Show captions when available
Renders an English subtitle/CC track when available. Takes effect on the next tune.
