Capture stream

The stream from your HDMI capture device. If it's set up in Channels DVR, use Find from Channels DVR.
No camera source in Channels DVR yet?
Create a native USB/HDMI capture source in Channels DVR, then FastChannels will select its exported stream automatically. Confirm the new capture channel plays in Channels before relying on it for bridge playback.
Uses capture://v4l2/video0/hw:1,0/?framerate=60. Device names are from the computer running Channels DVR, not the FastChannels container.
Check capture quality
Samples the saved capture stream and reports its format, whether it keeps up with real time, the frame rate that actually arrives, and (on the longer test) audio/video drift. Channels DVR does the capture and transcode, so this shows what it delivers, not why. It uses the capture port, so it won't run while Channels DVR is playing or recording.
Need to paste it manually instead? In Channels DVR, open Manage → Export → Copy M3U, open that URL, then copy the non-comment /stream.mpg line directly below your capture channel's #EXTINF entry. Paste it above and save.
