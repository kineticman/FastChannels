FastChannels Player devices
Every Fire TV / Android TV device running the player — live status and one-click updates
Enable hardware capture
Enables the FastChannels Player companion app used by HDMI Capture and ah4c Capture on every device; it switches on automatically with either of them, and switching it off pauses both. It only applies to verified DRM sources: Sling, NBCUniversal TVE, PBS, Amazon Prime (free), DirecTV Stream, Vidaa, Philo, Roku, and Fubo.
Show captions when available
Renders an English subtitle/CC track when available. Takes effect on the next tune.
Devices are listed from the HDMI Capture IP, ah4c's tuner list, anything added here, and any device FastChannels has tuned. Updates install the player bundled with this FastChannels version over ADB.
