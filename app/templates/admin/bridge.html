Choose a bridge method
Bridge mode keeps DRM-capable channels active for any enabled capture path. HDMI Capture and ah4c Capture use the FastChannels Player companion app on an Android TV or Fire TV device. When hardware capture and PrismCast Capture are both available, hardware capture is preferred for its verified sources.
ah4c Capture
Best for multi-tuner setups

Use this when several tuners or encoders may run at once. ah4c assigns each tune to its own device and capture path, so concurrent sessions do not steal one another’s player.

HDMI Capture
Single stream

Best for a budget, one-stream setup: one Android/Fire TV device and one fixed HDMI capture input. It cannot serve simultaneous channels.

PrismCast Capture
Browser capture

Choose this for hardware-free Chrome capture. PrismCast plays DRM in a browser and captures it in software, avoiding a dedicated device and HDMI encoder.
